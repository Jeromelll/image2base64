Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-15

Conversions stay on your device

Image ↔ Base64 tools on image2base64.com run entirely in your browser with the FileReader API. Images and Base64 strings are not uploaded to our servers.

What we may collect

  • Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics (privacy-oriented) may record aggregate page views. It does not use advertising cookies for cross-site tracking.
  • Feature usage events. To understand which tools people use, this site records anonymous, cookieless events (for example "an image was converted on this page", its coarse file-size range, or that a copy button was clicked). These events are stored on Cloudflare and contain no file contents, no file names, no IP addresses, no cookies, and no identifiers that could single out a visitor.
  • Hosting logs. Cloudflare may process standard request logs (IP, user agent, URL) for security and delivery.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no payment forms on this site.
  • We do not sell personal information.
