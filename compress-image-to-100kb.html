How this image compressor works

Your image is decoded and compressed on this device. The tool adjusts JPEG quality and, when needed, image dimensions until the downloaded file is at or below the selected byte limit. The displayed file size is checked before success is shown.

Compression uses JPEG, which is lossy and does not preserve transparency. Transparent pixels are filled with white. Animated WebP files become a single still image. Very small targets or complex images may not be achievable; choose a larger target and retry.
