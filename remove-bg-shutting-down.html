remove.bg Is Shutting Down on December 1, 2026

Yes — it's official. remove.bg has announced on its own homepage and FAQ that its background-removal feature is moving into Canva, and the standalone website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026 at 9:00am CET. Canva acquired remove.bg's maker Kaleido AI back in 2021, so this is a consolidation, not a closure of the technology — but it does retire one of the most-used free image tools on the web. Last updated: September 7, 2026.

What exactly was announced

The notice, shown across remove.bg's pages, states that the background-removal functionality is being migrated into Canva "so it sits alongside all the other features Canva offers", with the independent site switching off on the December date. The company points users to its updated General Terms and Conditions for the details that affect accounts, credits and subscriptions.

The scale of what is being retired is significant. On its own about page, remove.bg says it processes more than 150 million images per month for over 32 million monthly active users, including 240+ enterprise customers. Third-party traffic estimators put the site at roughly 40–60 million visits per month, with the majority coming from searches like "remove bg" and "background remover" — demand that will have to go somewhere after December 1.

Why this matters beyond the headline

remove.bg's free tier was unusually useful: unlimited background removals with a low-resolution (about 0.25 MP) transparent PNG download, no account required. That made it the default quick tool for product thumbnails, classroom worksheets, avatars, social posts and one-off design tasks. Canva's model is different — its pricing page lists background remover among Canva Pro premium tools, and its help docs describe limited AI allowances for free accounts. "The feature moved" is not the same as "the free service moved".

What remove.bg users should do before December 1

  1. Check your credits and their expiry. Purchased credits have expiration dates, and handling of unused balances during the migration is governed by the updated terms. Don't assume credits transfer indefinitely — verify on the official FAQ for your account and region.
  2. Review recurring billing. If you have an active subscription, confirm what happens at the migration date rather than assuming it cancels, transfers or renews on the terms you expect.
  3. Download anything stored in the service. Save processed images and assets locally; don't leave the only copy inside a service that is being retired.
  4. Test your replacement workflow early. If your work depends on quick cutouts — e-commerce listings, marketing assets, classroom materials — trial alternatives in October or November, not on December 1. Our remove.bg alternatives comparison maps the realistic options by use case.
  5. API users: plan a real migration. If your pipeline calls the remove.bg API, confirm with Canva how API access continues, and benchmark at least one fallback (open-source rembg, or a paid API such as Photoroom's or Slazzer's) before the cutover.

Where the traffic goes next

Some of remove.bg's audience will follow the feature into Canva. A large share — especially users who only ever wanted one free transparent PNG, not a design suite — will search for a replacement. Comparison pages and free-tier tools are already competing for that demand; we track the credible options, with their free-tier limits and pricing, on our remove.bg alternatives page.

Frequently asked questions

Is remove.bg really shutting down?

Yes. remove.bg has announced on its own website that its background-removal feature is moving into Canva, and that the standalone remove.bg website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026 at 9:00am CET. The announcement appears on the remove.bg homepage and FAQ page.

When exactly does remove.bg close?

The standalone website stops being available on 1 December 2026 at 9:00am Central European Time. After that point, the background-removal feature is offered inside Canva rather than as a separate website.

Will background removal still be free after the move to Canva?

Not in the same way. Canva lists background remover among its Canva Pro premium tools, and its help documentation describes limited AI usage for free accounts. That differs from remove.bg's long-standing free tier of unlimited low-resolution downloads, so users who relied on free cutouts should expect to need an alternative or a paid plan.

What happens to my remove.bg credits or subscription?

Check remove.bg's official FAQ and its updated General Terms and Conditions for the authoritative answer — credit expiry and subscription handling are set by Canva Austria GmbH, and the details can change before the shutdown date. As a general rule: spend remaining credits before 1 December 2026, review any recurring billing now, and download any files you still need.

What is the best free alternative to remove.bg?

It depends on your workflow. For genuinely free full-resolution downloads, Adobe Express (free account required) and Pixlr are the most generous options; for e-commerce catalogs, Photoroom and Pixelcut lead; for developers, open-source rembg runs locally with no per-image cost.
