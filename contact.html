Contact

Email: jeromell@be-winner.com

This is a one-person site. There is no phone line, no ticket form, and no chat widget. Email is the right channel for tool bugs, factual corrections, privacy questions, and advertising.

Corrections and tool bugs

Please include:

  • The page URL
  • What you expected and what actually happened
  • Browser and OS if it is a converter bug
  • The suggested fix, if you have one

Do not attach confidential images. Converters already run locally; a description of the format and the error text is enough.

Advertising

Partnership or ad-quality questions can use the same address. The site uses Google AdSense. We do not ask visitors to click ads, and we cannot “approve” a click on a specific creative. Cookie details: privacy policy. Seller authorization: ads.txt.

What we will not do

  • Decode or inspect a file you upload by email — there is no server pipeline for that.
  • Add a guest post, sponsored tutorial, or paid backlink.
